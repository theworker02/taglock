# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

TagLock welcomes people of all backgrounds and experience levels. Participants
are expected to communicate with respect, review ideas rather than identities,
accept technical disagreement in good faith, and help keep collaboration safe
and useful.

Examples of constructive participation include:

- Providing reproducible technical evidence.
- Explaining tradeoffs without dismissing other contributors.
- Respecting privacy, attribution, and project security boundaries.
- Correcting mistakes openly and helping others learn.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized
attention, deliberate disruption, publication of private information, abuse of
reporting channels, or retaliation against someone raising a concern.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions,
community spaces, and private project communication. It also applies when a
participant publicly represents TagLock or theworker02.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Do not open a public
issue containing personal or sensitive information. Reports will be reviewed
in context and shared only as needed to investigate and respond.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, issue a warning, temporarily
restrict participation, or permanently remove a participant when necessary.
Good-faith reports will not result in retaliation. Deliberately false or
malicious reports may themselves violate this policy.

## Maintainer responsibility

Maintainers should apply this policy consistently, explain enforcement actions
when privacy permits, and separate conduct decisions from ordinary technical
disagreement.
